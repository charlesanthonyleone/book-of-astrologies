Subject: Charles Leone, born 13 October 1998, 21:25 UT, Johnstown, Pennsylvania (40.33 N, 78.92 W). Labels: Calculated = computed in this chapter (Skyfield DE440s, Phugpa arithmetic); Source, verify = taken from a published tradition source whose tables vary; Interpretation = the author's reading.

Tibetan astrology (tsi, "calculation") has two halves. Kar-tsi ("white calculation") is the Indian-derived astronomy of the Kalachakra Tantra: planets, lunar mansions and the calendar. Jung-tsi ("elemental calculation") is the Chinese-derived system of animals, elements, mewa and parkha. Source, verify A traditional chart-maker (tsipa) uses both together, and so does this chapter.

4. Outer, inner and alternative Kalachakra

The Kalachakra teaches that the outer cosmos (planets, months), the inner body (channels, winds, drops) and the "alternative" enlightened form are three aspects of one wheel. Source, verify In the inner Kalachakra, the 12 zodiac signs correspond to 12 shifts of breath through the right (roma, Sun) and left (kyangma, Moon) channels each day, and the central channel (uma) is linked to Rahu. Source, verify Your Sun is hidden in H8 and your Moon is in its own sign, so the left, lunar channel is the stronger of the pair in your chart. Interpretation
