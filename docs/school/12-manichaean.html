Manichaeism does not cast horoscopes in order to obey the stars. Mani taught that the zodiac and the planets are archons, powers of Darkness that the Living Spirit bound to the firmament and set turning as a wheel (Keph. 47, 69). The wheel works like a water-wheel or sieve: its 12 vessels lift the light freed from the world up to the Moon-ship. From the Moon the light passes to the Sun-ship and then home to the Realm of Light. So a Manichaean reading asks three questions. Which dark worlds hold your planets? What state is the light-transfer in (the Moon phase)? Which calendar of prayer and fasting frames your birth? Source

Two worlds get three signs. Five stars came from these worlds: Zeus from Smoke, Aphrodite from Fire, Ares from Wind, Hermes from Water and Kronos from Darkness.
