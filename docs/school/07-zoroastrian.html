Mars (Wahram) is in Leo (Sher) in H7, conjunct the Gochihr head. The Moon (Mah) is in Cancer (Kalachang) in H6. Saturn (Kewan) is retrograde in Aries (Varak) in H3. The Lot of Fortune is in Sagittarius (Nimasp) at 13.7° in H11.

House walk-through (Calculated placement, Interpretation meaning):

  • H1, Aquarius/Dul (Jupiter): you lead with the creator's own planet.
  • H3, Aries (Saturn Rx): slow, stubborn work in speech, siblings and short journeys. It is a growth house, so Saturn improves over time.
  • H6, Cancer (Moon): feeling goes into service and daily labour.
  • H7, Leo (Mars and the Gochihr head): partners and open rivals are where heat and contest happen.

What this means for you: you are built around a rising creator-planet that is almost standing still. The tradition reads that as steadiness and a sense of being placed on purpose. Your practical tests come through partnership (H7) and your daily duties (H6). Your growth comes through study and belief (H9).

What this means for you: your emotional nature sits in the rain-maker's mansion. In this lore that means the one who brings relief after drought. Interpretation You tend to help after a crisis, not before it.

5. The 12,000-year ages and millennium lords

Source, verify The 12,000 years are split into four ages of 3,000 (Bd 1, 34). The last three millennia belong to Zarathustra, Ushedar and Ushedarmah. Each millennium has a zodiac sign as its lord. Calculated 1998 falls in the 12th millennium (Ushedarmah) under either chronology:

9. The Pahlavi nativity texts and bakht

Source, verify The Pahlavi texts (Mx 24, 27; Dk) contrast bakht, fixed fate from the stars, with kunishn, action. Fate governs wealth, children, rule and lifespan. Effort governs righteousness and the soul. The Persian nativity method uses firdar periods.

The 13th-child marker (born the 13th, youngest of 13)

Calculated Sidereal (Lahiri) chart: lagna Kumbha 26°48' with Jupiter rising at 25°52'; 3rd house Mesha holding the lagna lord Saturn Rx (Ashvini); 3rd lord Mars in the 7th (Simha); dispositor chain Mars → Sun (Kanya, 8th) → Mercury (Tula, 9th) ⇄ Venus (Kanya), closing in the Mercury–Venus exchange with Venus in Hasta, the 13th nakshatra; 11th lord Jupiter rising.

Interpretation What he carries. The last-born saviour is the one after the twelve thousand years; he is the one after the twelve. His 3rd, the youngest's house, holds the lagna lord, so the last-born and the self coincide, and the 3rd lord's chain ends in the 13th lunar station, Hasta.

14. Integrated Reading

Interpretation You were born in the tradition's last millennium, on the sky's day (Asman), in Mithra's month, during the harvest feast, at the afternoon watch of the waters. Ohrmazd's planet stands almost still on your Ascendant. That is the chart's dominant fact: a steady, principled presence that others feel as protection.

Your rain-mansion Moon makes you the one who restores things after a dry spell. Your Mercury in the house of religion makes learning and belief your field of growth. The one robber to manage is Mars on the dragon's head in the partnership house. Contained, it becomes the Keresaspa image: strength used to bind harm, not to cause it.

The prophecy figures do not describe your fate. Their symbols (water, a standing light, the bound dragon) are a vocabulary for your conduct. Bakht has been dealt; kunishn is yours.
