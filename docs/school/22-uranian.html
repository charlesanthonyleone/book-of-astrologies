22.1 Sources and method

Source The Hamburg School was set up as the Astrologenverein Hamburger Schule on 31 Oct 1925. Witte posited Cupido, Hades, Zeus and Kronos; Sieggrün added Apollon, Admetos, Vulkanus and Poseidon (1927). The Regelwerk für Planetenbilder (Witte/Rudolph 1928; later editions by Lefeldt, Pluto and Sieggrün's points added) is the school's book of rules for reading planetary pictures. Sources: Wikipedia, "Hamburg School of Astrology" (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg_School_of_Astrology); Udo Rudolph / M. Feist, "The Hamburg School" (uraniansociety.com/USIG_articles/article_history_of_uranian_astrology_michael_feist.pdf); Hamburger Schule e.V., "Grundlagen – Transneptuner" (hamburgerschule.de); Witte-Verlag listing of the Witte–Lefeldt Regelwerk (witte-verlag.com); astronova.de listing of the Regelwerk (7th ed. 2012). Ebertin: Kombination der Gestirneinflüsse (1940; English The Combination of Stellar Influences, AFA). Ebertin's medical meanings are left out of this chapter on purpose.

Calculated Planets are geocentric and apparent, ecliptic of date, from JPL DE440s via Skyfield 1.55. The node is the mean node, which is the school's custom. The MC and ASC come from GAST. The TNPs come from the Witte/Sieggrün elements as refined by James Neely, listed in the Swiss Ephemeris file seorbel.txt (github.com/aloistr/swisseph/ephe/seorbel.txt). Epoch and equinox are J1900. A Kepler solution gives the heliocentric position, it is precessed to date, and then converted to geocentric. Orbs follow the school: Witte's pictures within 1°, Ebertin's midpoint trees within 1°30′, sums within 0°15′, solar arcs within 0°01′ of exact (true solar arc, progressed Sun minus natal Sun).

A note on reading. The Hamburg School has no dignities, rulerships or aspect doctrine beyond the 90°/45°/22.5° harmonics. A factor is strong when it sits on a personal point or forms a tight picture, and every result is read as a sum of meanings (A+B−C=D). "What this means for you" paragraphs are hand-written Interpretation, one per item, checked against the keyword sources in 22.15. Wording from memory is flagged verify.

Moon frame (his feelings). Mars and the Node in the Moon's house 1 mean his feelings act at once and turn into contacts. Synthesis: In the Meridian frame the Sun, Apollon and Venus are in house 10 (Sun 200° − MC 265° → house 10). Self, breadth and pleasure are placed right in the career field of his own aims. In the Earth frame the Libra group falls in house 7, so the world meets him through partners and the public.
