Born 13 October 1998, 21:25 UT, Johnstown, Pennsylvania (40.33°N, 78.92°W). Tropical zodiac, Skyfield/DE421, true positions in the ecliptic of date, mean Node. Houses are equal houses from the Ascendant, which is the scheme Alan Leo uses in his worked examples. The MC is a separate point.

Theosophical astrology grew out of the Theosophical Society, founded in 1875. Helena Blavatsky laid down the cosmology: cycles, races, the seven principles and the coming avatars (The Secret Doctrine, 1888). Alan Leo turned it into a working astrology in which the horoscope maps the soul's growth (Esoteric Astrology, 1913). Charubel and Sepharial, both Theosophists, published clairvoyantly received degree symbols. Alice Bailey built the fullest system: the seven rays, esoteric and hierarchical rulers, the three crosses and the soul-centred chart (Esoteric Astrology, 1951). This chapter uses all of those layers. It is a symbolic reading in the tradition's own terms. Nothing here claims a destiny.

1. Alan Leo: the planets as principles

Leo (Esoteric Astrology, ch. III) gives each planet a principle of the human constitution. In his scheme the Sun is the Individuality, the reincarnating Ego. The Moon is the Personality, the self of this one life. The Ascendant is the physical expression.

It is the point where the soul turns from instinct toward relationship and balance. Your Individuality is in a cardinal air sign and sits in the house of partners. What this means for you: the core of who you are, in Leo's sense, is a self that becomes itself through other people. You find out who you are by weighing, choosing and pairing up, not by going it alone. The Sun is within 4° of Venus, the ruler of Libra, and Venus is strong in her own sign. That gives your Individuality a natural grace, a feel for fairness, and an eye for what is beautiful and right.

Moon in Leo 7°31', 5th house: the Personality. The Moon here is in the Sun's own sign and the Sun's own natural house. The personality is warm, proud and creative, and it wants to shine. 2. Bailey: rays, esoteric rulers and the soul-centred chart

In Bailey's scheme the rising sign is the soul's purpose, the Sun sign is the personality's equipment, and the Moon shows the past, the form the soul has outgrown. Each sign has an orthodox ruler for the ordinary person, an esoteric ruler for the disciple, and a hierarchical ruler for the initiate. These come from Tabulation VI, checked against the Lucis Trust online text.

Pisces rising. - Orthodox ruler: Jupiter, rising in Pisces. - Esoteric and hierarchical ruler: Pluto, in Sagittarius 6°13' in the 9th house, trine the Moon at 1°18'.

Bailey's soul keynote for Pisces is "I leave the Father's Home and turning back, I save." Pisces is the sign of the World Saviour (EA, "Pisces"). What this means for you: in Bailey's language your soul's purpose is service and redemption: going back for others. Its esoteric ruler, Pluto, is first ray. It sits in the house of philosophy and long journeys and makes a near-exact trine to your Moon. That means the purpose works by cutting away old forms, and it reaches the old self (the Moon) smoothly, not violently.

Sun in Libra. - Orthodox ruler: Venus, in Libra. - Esoteric ruler: Uranus, in Aquarius. - Hierarchical ruler: Saturn, in Taurus.

Keynote: "I choose the way which leads between the two great lines of force." What this means for you: all three rulers of your Sun sign are strong. Venus and Uranus are each in their own sign, and Saturn trines Mars, the Node and the MC. Bailey's Libra is "the hall of decision". Your personality equipment is built for weighing opposites and choosing the middle path, and it is well supplied at every level.

Moon in Leo. All three Leo rulers are the Sun. Bailey says that in Leo the Moon "veils" Vulcan or Neptune. Keynote: "I am That and That am I." What this means for you: your past is one of strong self-consciousness, a fully formed "I". Leo's soul keynote holds the key to dissolving that past: knowing that the self and the One are the same.

MC in Sagittarius. The esoteric ruler is Earth, which sits in Sagittarius opposite the Sun in your 9th-to-8th zone. The hierarchical ruler is Mars, in Virgo. What this means for you: the public role is linked to Earth itself, to service on the ground. It is carried out through Mars in Virgo's careful work.

Rays. These are the ray qualities of your placements. Bailey says the soul ray and personality ray cannot simply be read off the chart, so treat this as the ray "climate" of your chart. - Ray 2 (Love-Wisdom): Pisces rising, Jupiter rising, and the Sun. - Ray 1 (Will): Pluto as soul ruler, and Leo. - Ray 3 (Intelligence): the Libra Sun sign and Saturn. - Ray 7 (Order, ritual): Uranus, the esoteric ruler of the Sun.

What this means for you: the strongest note is love-wisdom at the soul point, with will (ray 1) as the soul's instrument. Your character is teaching and saving, backed by the will to see things through.

The three crosses. - The Sun is on the Cardinal cross, which Bailey calls the Cross of the initiate. - The Moon is on the Fixed cross, the disciple's. - The Ascendant is on the Mutable cross, the "common" cross of experience.

What this means for you: Bailey's crosses are stages. They are not grades to boast about. Read symbolically, your chart touches all three. The soul point is on the cross of experience (Mutable). The past is on the disciple's cross (Fixed). The personality is on the cross that starts new cycles (Cardinal). Taken together, that is a full sweep.

Triangles (partial). Bailey links signs through their shared rays. - Pisces and Taurus share the first-ray link: Pluto rules Pisces and Vulcan rules Taurus. Your Saturn is in Taurus. - Libra and Gemini share ray 3.

What this means for you: your rising sign has a first-ray line to your Saturn, so your values (2nd house) give the will to serve something solid to stand on.

Vulcan. Vulcan is the esoteric ruler of Taurus. It is hypothetical and has no ephemeris in these sources, so it is not computed.

Synthesis, Bailey: a Pisces soul point, with the World-Saviour keynote, worked through a Libra personality that is fully supported by its three rulers. The past is in Leo self-consciousness. The ray mix is love-wisdom used through will. In Bailey's terms the chart shows a server's purpose.

4. Sepharial

Sepharial's Chaldean name-number for CHARLES LEONE works out as CHARLES 22 + LEONE 25 = 47, then 4 + 7 = 11, then 1 + 1 = 2. What this means for you: Sepharial reads 11 as a master number and 2 as the number of the Moon, of pairs and of balance. That echoes your Libra Sun.

Hebrew values (The Kabala of Numbers, p. 29). Sepharial's Hebraic unit table (1 = A, I, Y, Q, J; 2 = B, C, K, R; 3 = G, L, S; 4 = D, M, T; 5 = E, N; 6 = U, V, W, X; 7 = O, Z; 8 = F, P, H; 9 = Th, Tz) gives CHARLES 24 + LEONE 25 = 49, then 13, then 4. In his scheme 4 is the Earth or the Sun. Using full Hebrew letter-values instead, which is the method behind his "Napoleone = 361", CHARLES = 561 (3, Jupiter) and LEONE = 160 (7, the Full Moon), giving a total of 721, then 10, then 1, the Sun.

What this means for you: the Hebrew reckonings land on the Sun and on Jupiter, which is your rising planet. The name carries the same signature of solar self and expansive teaching that the chart does.

7f. Sepharial's lunar forecasting

Sepharial's Manual of Astrology (Book III, ch. V, "Lunations", and the Supplement) gives three lunar methods. 1. Lunations. A new moon on the MC, Ascendant, Sun, Moon or a planet marks an event within the following month. It brings on a direction of the same nature within about fourteen days, often at the next full moon, and holds back one of opposite nature. Two or three successive new moons on key places mark an epoch. On benefics the result is good, on malefics testing, and eclipses strengthen the effect. 2. The Lunar Equivalent. Using birth-time arithmetic, 2 hours equal one month and 4 minutes one day, so the progressed Moon's aspects can be timed to the month. 3. The Diurnal Horoscope. This is a chart set for the birth time on any day. Transits over its angles time the day, and the house of the month's new moon shows which area of life the month's changes touch.
