Thelema does not throw away the old astrology. Crowley learned it from the Golden Dawn and then spent two years writing a textbook with Evangeline Adams. What changes is the frame. Liber AL says "Every man and every woman is a star" (I:3). Each chart is one star moving through the body of Nuit, the night sky. The Sun-point at its centre is Hadit, the "flame that burns in every heart of man" (II:6). Every planet is then translated into the Qabalah of 777 and the images of the Thoth Tarot. Reading a chart in Thelema therefore means reading what a person's True Will looks like in the language of the stars.

1a. Rising sign, Sun and Moon (Crowley/Adams portraits)

  • Pisces rising, Jupiter on the degree. Astrology describes Pisces rising as receptive, intuitive and compassionate, "sensitive to every influence," and weak only when it has no strong ruler. Your ruler Jupiter is in its own sign, exactly rising. Thelema's rule is that a sign's weakness is cured by a dignified ruler. What this means for you: People meet you first as generous, kind and broad-minded. Your sympathy comes with Jupiter's backbone, so it does not dissolve; it enlarges. The Thoth image of the rising degree (Queen of Wands, below) adds steady fire to the water.
  • Sun in Libra, 7th house. Crowley's Libra is the sign of balance, justice and art. It is "the scales which weigh all things," and he gave it the trump Adjustment. What this means for you: Your Will (the Sun) works through equilibrium, fairness and partnership. You find yourself by weighing, matching and harmonising.
  • Moon in Leo, 5th house. The Leo Moon is warm, proud, theatrical and loyal, and needs to create and be seen. What this means for you: Your emotional life is generous and dramatic. It wants an audience, a love to celebrate and something made with the heart. In Thelema this is the Moon in Lust: joy taken as a sacrament.

1b. Uranus, Neptune, Pluto (Crowley's added essays)

  • Uranus 8°49' Aquarius, 11th, retrograde. Crowley wrote that Uranus is the planet of the magical will and of sudden genius. It is in its modern home sign and the house of friends and hopes. What this means for you: Original ideas come through groups and networks. Because Uranus is opposite your Moon, you are emotionally drawn to the unconventional.
  • Neptune 29°23' Capricorn, 11th. This is the anaretic last degree, on the cusp of Aquarius. In Crowley's reading Neptune is spiritual vision and the dissolution of forms. What this means for you: Your ideals about structures and institutions are ready to cross over into something new. You picture society's forms already changing.
  • Pluto 6°13' Sagittarius, 9th (a later addition, since the text predates Pluto). What this means for you: Deep transformation runs through belief, travel, teaching and philosophy. It is exactly trine your Moon, so the change comes as inspiration rather than crisis.
