2. Mandaean astrology: Asfar Malwasia (Drower, The Book of the Zodiac, 1949) Source, verify

The Asfar Malwasia is the priests' handbook of the zodiac. It is used to name children through the malwasha, to choose days, and to read omens. Its content descends from Babylonian and Hellenistic sources, and it was put into Mandaic dress. Drower's 1949 translation (Oriental Translation Fund, vol. 36) is the standard edition. No archive.org copy of the Book of the Zodiac or the Diwan Abatur could be found online for this pass. The volume is catalogued by the BnF and Open Library, and Mandaean community copies are posted at egnu93.wordpress.com. The head/loins/tail lords below were checked against the local text copy at /workspace/gno13/drower.txt.

2.2 The malwasha (baptismal name-horoscope) Source, verify; Calculated

Method (Drower 1949; also Mandaeans of Iraq and Iran, ch. 6): 1. Take the value 1–12 of the mother's baptismal name, from the letter tables. 2. Begin from the sign of the birth month (Embra) and count the hour of birth to find the hour-sign. 3. Subtract the mother's number from the hour-sign value, mod 12. The result is the child's sign. 4. The priest then chooses the child's baptismal name from the names listed under that sign.

2.3 Zodiac omens: the man born in Embra (Aries) and the man born in Qaina (Libra) Source, verify

Book 1 of the Asfar Malwasia reads a man born in each month through twelve "houses", which are derived from his sign. For the Embra man the book describes: - a man of action and early rise; - conflict over property; - authority that comes through effort; - a life lived through journeys.

For the Qaina (Libra) man it describes: - dealings with weighing, trade and partners; - a balanced temper; - gains that come through others.

These are paraphrases; check them against the text before quoting. The old house headings for the 6th and 8th houses are rendered here neutrally as the "6th place" and "8th place".

What this means for you: Read the Embra text as your social and ritual identity and the Qaina text as your solar nature. Where the two agree (gain through partners, a life of movement), the reading is strongest. Your Sun and Venus sit in the 8th place, which is other people's resources, and that matches "gains through others". Interpretation

2.6 The seven planets and twelve signs as demons (Ginza Right 3; Book of John) Source, verify

In Mandaean myth the seven planets (shiba) and twelve signs (trisar) are the children of Ruha (the fallen spirit) and Ur. Their ruler is Ptahil-Ruha's world. They are the "liars" who lead souls astray. Each planet bears a false-prophet role: - Shamish (Sun) is linked to Adonai and the Jews. - Nbu (Mercury) is linked to Christ (the "false messiah"). - Libat (Venus) is linked to Ishtar. - Sin (Moon), Kiwan (Saturn), Bil (Jupiter) and Nirig (Mars) have their own cult roles.

The twelve signs are the archons' "army".

What this means for you: The demon reading points in a direction opposite to normal astrology. Your strong Bil is still a ruler of this world. The Mandaean ideal is to know him without being owned by him. Read your Jupiter-rising as a great gift that must not become your god. Interpretation

3. Gnostic astrology

3.1 Pistis Sophia: the Sphere of Fate, the 12 aeons and the turning of the archons Source, verified online at sacred-texts.com, ps019

Book I, ch. 15, in Mead's translation, says: "I took from all a third of their power... And the Fate and the sphere over which they rule, I have changed and brought it to pass that they spend six months turned to the left... and six months they face to the right." Before this change, Yew had set them always facing left.

Two other ideas from the text matter here: - Heimarmene is the sphere of fate. - The 12 aeons are ruled by Adamas.

The text makes astrologers' predictions half-reliable: they hold true only when the archons face left.

Interpretation The text does not define when the archons face left or right. This chapter takes it that the zodiac half the Sun occupies is the "face". Your Sun was in Libra, in the autumn half. Under the 1/3 rule, every archon influence on you operates at two-thirds strength. That includes the Mercury–Saturn opposition and the Moon–Saturn square.

What this means for you: This tradition hands you a built-in discount on fate: the hardest contacts in your chart are real but not total. Treat them as tendencies that you can work, not sentences. Interpretation

3.2 Apocryphon of John: the 365 angels of the body Source, verify: NHC II,1 15–19

The Apocryphon of John says the seven rulers and their 365 angels built the psychic body of Adam limb by limb. The text names them in order: for example Eteraphaope-Abron for the head, Tebar for the right shoulder, Agromauna for the heart, Sorma for the genitals, and Miamai for the toenails.

This chapter maps each body part onto its traditional melothesia sign and so onto your planets. Calculated mapping; Interpretation - Saturn is on the neck (Adaban) and the throat angels around it (Basiliademe, Achcha, Dearcho). Mercury opposes it. - Moon and Node are on the heart (Agromauna). - Mars is on the belly, navel, liver and spleen (Areche, Phthave, Sostrapal, Anesimalar). - Sun and Venus are on the hips and kidneys (Barias, Phnouth, Biblo). - Mercury is on Eilo and Sorma. - The MC (no classical planet) is on the thighs (Gorma-Kaiochlabar, Nebrith). - Capricorn and Aquarius are empty: the knees (Achiel, Phnene) and the legs and shins (Ormaoth, Emenun, Knyx, Tupelon) carry none of the seven. - Spelling corrections from NHC II,1 (gnosis.org): Akioreim, Asterechme, Thaspomocha, Beluai, Agromauna. Achiel/Phnene are the knees and Ormaoth/Emenun the legs; the earlier draft had them swapped. - Jupiter and the ASC are on the feet and toes (Phiouthrom, Boabel, Trachoun, Phikna).

What this means for you: The angels who would "speak" most in your psychic body are at the throat (Saturn) and the heart (Moon). Gnostically, your work is to give voice to what the heart knows without the throat-archon censoring it. The feet angels carry Jupiter, so moving and walking are where you are blessed. Interpretation

3.3 The Ophite diagram (Origen, Contra Celsum VI.24–38) Source, verify

Origen describes the diagram as ten circles, bound by a serpent Leviathan, with seven archons with animal faces as gatekeepers. Verified online (newadvent.org, ANF vol. 4): in VI.30 the first, lion-like archon is Michael, and Origen adds that the star Phaenon (Saturn) is said to be in sympathy with the lion-like ruler. The second is the bull (Suriel), the third the serpent (Raphael), then the eagle (Gabriel), the bear (Thauthabaoth), the dog (Erataoth) and the ass (Onoel or Thartharaoth). VI.31 gives the passwords spoken to Ialdabaoth, Iao, Sabaoth, Astaphaeus, Aloaeus and Horaeus. VI.25 names Leviathan, Behemoth and Gehenna on the diagram. Correction: the earlier draft said Origen calls Ialdabaoth lion-faced; that lion-faced serpent is in the Apocryphon of John, not Origen. In Origen the lion is Michael, linked to Saturn.

What this means for you: The ladder of the diagram runs from Saturn (Phaenon, in sympathy with the lion-like Michael) at the top down to the Moon. Your Saturn is retrograde and tightly bound to Mercury, so the lion-archon is the first gate you must answer. In plain terms that gate is authority, structure and fear of judgement. Interpretation

3.8 The Books of Jeu: seals, names and treasuries Source, verified: MacDermot/Schmidt 1978 edition of the Bruce Codex; Calculated; Interpretation

What the text says. The First Book of Jeu describes 60 treasuries of Jeu. Each has a seal (a drawn diagram), a name and a cipher number. The rite at each one is the same: "seal yourselves with this seal", say its name once while holding its cipher in your hand, then say the name three times, and its watchers, ranks and veils draw back so that you may pass. Each treasury has "six places" around it and "twelve heads" or ranks inside. Most of the names are cryptograms that the edition prints only as letters or dots, and several seal drawings are lost from the manuscript. Only some ciphers survive legibly, for example the 55th to 59th: 600515, 90410, 90419, 70122 and 90187 (all marked uncertain by the editors). Source

The Second Book of Jeu (ch. 52) gives the ciphers for the thirteen aeons the soul crosses: 1 = 1119, 2 = 2219, 3 = 3349 (the aeon of Jaldabaoth), 4 = 4555 (Samaelo), 5 = 5369, 6 = 6915, 7 = 7889, 8 = 8054, 9 = 2889, 10 = 4559, 11 = 5558, 12 = 9885 (the invisible God with Barbelo) and 13 = 8855. Source

Method for your chart. Jeu gives no rule for linking a treasury to a birth chart. This chapter divides the 360° zodiac into 60 parts of 6°, one per treasury (treasury = longitude ÷ 6, rounded down, plus 1). Calculated For the aeon gates it takes the seven planets in Chaldean order from the Moon upward as gates 1–7. This conflicts with Jeu, which places Jaldabaoth (usually Saturn) at aeon 3, so treat it as a mapping only. Interpretation
