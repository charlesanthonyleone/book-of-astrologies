  • Signs: He = Aries (V) through Qoph = Pisces.
  • Thoth (Mayananda's base): the letters He and Tzaddi are swapped between IV Emperor and XVII Star, while the sign attributions stay the same (Aries Emperor, Aquarius Star). VIII and XI are swapped as Adjustment and Lust; XIV is Art.
  • Wirth (tableau in Papus, Tarot of the Bohemians, ch. XVI, 1889, https://sacred-texts.com/tarot/tob/tob44.htm; repeated in Wirth, Le Tarot des imagiers, 1927, ch. 4, https://archive.org/details/TarotOfTheMagiciansByOswaldWirth): Aries V, Taurus I, Gemini XIX, Cancer XVIII, Leo XI, Virgo III, Libra VIII, Scorpio XVI, Sagittarius VI, Capricorn X, Aquarius XIV, Pisces XVII.
  • Minors (Book T):
  • Decans run in Chaldean order from Aries I = Mars; pips 2–10 from Aries (Wands) onward; each pip carries its Sephira.
  • Courts span 20° of one sign to 20° of the next.
  • Papus instead gives spring decans to Batons, summer to Cups, autumn to Swords and winter to Coins.

What this means for you: Different schools give different cards for the same planet, and that tells you something. Where they all agree, the meaning is certain. Your Sun gives Justice in three schools and Wirth's Justice in a fourth. The French Hanged Man is the only dissent, and it adds depth rather than contradicting. Synthesis: Read across the systems, your chart is a chart of balance (Libra Sun and Venus), courage (the Leo Moon as Strength) and grace (Jupiter on the Ascendant as Wheel and 9 of Cups).

Read through him, your Sun arcanum XII (Pendu) is "the Sacrifice", and your Moon arcanum IX (Ermite) is "Initiation/Prudence". That makes you the initiate who serves.
