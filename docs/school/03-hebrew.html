The Gra text lists these three faculties exactly so. What this means for you. Lamed is the tallest letter, and its root means learning and teaching (lamad/limmed). Your Sun letter points to a life organised around learning and around union. Tet (the Moon) makes your emotional faculty hearing: you take in the world by listening. Qof (the rising sign) makes laughter the faculty you show. Together they describe someone who teaches, listens and lightens a room. The same letters also carry their shadow: tashmish untamed is appetite, and laughter can become mockery (letzanut). The school asks for both to be sanctified, not suppressed.
