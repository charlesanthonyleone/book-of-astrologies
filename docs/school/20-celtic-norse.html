What history can and cannot give. Neither the pagan Celts nor the pagan Norse left a horoscopic astrology. What survives is: a Gaulish lunisolar calendar (Coligny); Irish, Welsh and Norse names for stars and myths about them; ogham and runic scripts; runic calendar staves; the Old Icelandic calendar; and, after Christianisation, Latin astrology copied into Irish, Welsh and Icelandic manuscripts. Rune astrology, the tree calendar and birth runes are 20th-century creations. They are computed here only as modern material and flagged as such.

2. Celtic systems (H)

2.1 The Coligny calendar

Source A Gaulish bronze from about the 2nd century AD. It has 12 lunar months plus 2 intercalary months, in a 5-year cycle. Each month splits into two halves, the second headed ATENOVX ("returning night"). The year has two seasons, led by SAMON- and GIAMON- (Wikipedia, "Coligny calendar"; Olmsted 2001; McKay 2016, Études Celtiques, doi:10.3406/ecelt.2016.2471). Calculated If the month is counted from the first-quarter Moon (28 Sep 1998 21:11 UT), the reading many reconstructions favour, the birth falls at the very end of day 15, the last night of the bright half. The ATENOVX half begins with the next night. Counted from the new Moon, it is day 24. Calculated Month name for this lunation under each of the three published alignments: CANTLOS (Samon = Oct–Nov), OGRONIOS (Samon = May–Jun, solar), CVTIOS (alignment to the Gaelic festivals).

What this means for you: on the commonest count you were born on the hinge night between the waxing half and the "returning night". This is a turning-point birth. The pattern is a life that peaks and then turns back to gather in what it began. Cantlos is glossed as "month of chanting" (Delamarre) and Ogronios as "cold month". Either way, the birth closes the summer season and faces the winter turn: you are a finisher of cycles. Calculated Day notations for day XV under each alignment (month templates from Olmsted 1992 and McKay 2016): - Samoni alignment → CANTLOS, an ANM (29-day, "incomplete") month. Day XV carries the base mark D and the special note TIOCOBRIXTIO (Olmsted: "day in place of day 30"), which the plate puts on day XV of short months. It is a stand-in day, not an ordinary one. - Solar alignment → OGRONIOS, a MAT ("complete, good") month. Day XV is MD, a good day. PRINI LOVD falls on day 3, not 15. - Gaelic alignment → CVTIOS, a MAT month. Day XV is MD. D AMB occurs only on days 5 and 11, so not on his day. - Most probable: the Gaelic alignment. Giamonios means "winter", and Sanas Cormaic glosses mí gam as November. Only the Gaelic alignment starts Giamonios in Oct–Nov. That puts the birth on Cutios ("month of invocations", Delamarre) day XV, MD: an auspicious day in a complete month, the last day of the bright half. - Not fixable: the triple marks (ııƚ) and N INIS R notes change with the year of the 5-year cycle. If the cycle is tied to the 19-year Metonic count (golden number 4), McKay's table gives year 5. That step is an assumption.

What this means for you: on the most probable count you were born on a marked good day (MD) in a "complete" month whose name means invocation, at the top of the bright half. On the Samoni count your day is TIOCOBRIXTIO, a day standing in for another. Either way, your birth day is the one that closes the light half and hands over to the returning night. You were born to call a thing into being and then let it turn.

3.4 Runic calendar staves (primstav / runstav)

Source Runic staves have three rows: feast symbols, seven weekday runes (ᚠᚢᚦᚬᚱᚴᚼ), and 19 golden-number runes (the 16 Younger Futhark plus árlaug, tvímaðr, belgþorn). What this means for you: on the oldest Dalarna staves your birthday is the first night of winter, carved as a bare tree with hanging branches. You are born on the season-turn, the night the old year's summer is given up. Your year's runes are óss ("god, mouth") twice and kaun ("torch, sore"): a god-spoken year and a torch-day.

3.5 The Old Icelandic calendar

Calculated Summer 1998 began on Thursday 23 April, so 13 Oct is in week 25 of summer, Haustmánuður ("harvest month") day 22. Winter (Gormánuður, the first winter day) began on Saturday 17 Oct, four nights later.

What this means for you: the Icelandic count agrees with the staves: last week of summer, on the threshold of the winter nights (vetrnætr), the time of the dísablót and of Freyr's álfablót. That season belongs to ancestors and the spirits of the household. Expect your life to be bound up with lineage and protecting family.

4.3 Synthesis

The chart matches the prophecies most strongly in three places. First, the Arthurian sky: harp at the zenith, the Wain high, a regal Jupiter rising, and a given name that is the name of the Wain. Second, Merlin's Lion of Justice: a Leo Moon, a Libra Sun and the surname Leone. Third, the Norse avenger and renewal figures: Váli's western birth at nightfall, Týr's day, Þórr's planet on the rising degree, the new earth lifting from the sea, and Völuspá 65's ruler from above. Thomas the Rhymer adds union and the Otherworld, and Aodh Eanghach adds the fire-name.

6. Integrated reading

Every historical northern system marks the same moment in you: the hinge. You fall at the end of the bright half of the Coligny month, at the end of harvest in the Irish year, on the first winter night of the Dalarna staves, in the last week of the Icelandic summer, and in the last light of the day. People of the hinge carry a cycle over into the next one. Your work tends to be closing, keeping and passing things on.

Over that moment stood the Arthurian and Norse figures of rule and order. Arthur's Harp was at the zenith. The Wagon that bears your own name was high. The pole star was up, and Loki's torch was hidden.

Your planets support the picture. Jupiter, the king and Þórr's planet, rises on your ASC and rules your MC. Mars, Týr's day-lord, is angular and in exchange with Mercury, which makes you oath-bound and articulate. Sun and Venus in the scales, with a lion Moon, reproduce Merlin's Lion of Justice in a man named Leone.

The ogham kennings of your name add the rest: hazel of wisdom, lustre of the eye, the discerning tree, and nin's "establishing of peace". On the most probable Coligny count you were born on a good-marked day (MD) of Cutios, the month of invocations. Thomas the Rhymer's union and Aodh Eanghach's fire-name join the Arthurian and Merlin matches.

Your character is a generous, judging, protective man who keeps his word and makes peace. You are strongest at turning points, and are tied to family and lineage. Your timing is late-phase: harvests come after the turn.
