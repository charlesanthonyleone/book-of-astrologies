2. Tzolk'in: 8 Kaban (K'iche' 8 No'j)

  • Tzolk'in: 8 Kaban Calculated. Under 584285 it would be 6 Men.
  • Position in the 260-day count: day 177 of 260.

What this means for you. Kaban is the Earth sign: earth, earthquake and movement. Its Aztec equivalent is Ollin. The Chilam Balam text on the Ollin day says the person is "a wise merchant, a walker, a bloodletter and healer, good and prudent, a good carpenter". Its emblem is the woodpecker Source, verify.

Durán says that "men born on this sign would shine like the sun". La Serna says that on this day "valiant captains were born" Source, verify.

The number 8 is the number of Waqxaqib' B'atz', the K'iche' day on which day-keepers are initiated. That makes it a strong, balanced number Source: Tedlock 1982.

Taken together, the reading is a grounded and practical mind that moves things and people. He is someone whose ideas shake ground. Interpretation

Placement-first / hidden strength (this tradition's logic). This system has no "debilitated" day; each day is judged by three things: 1. its number: low numbers 1–3 are gentle, high numbers 10–13 are forceful, and the middle numbers are balanced; 2. its lord; 3. the trecena that frames it.

On that basis: - 8 is a balanced middle number. - The Ollin sign is double-edged in Sahagún: "indifferent" fortune, valiant if well raised. - Verdict: strong-in-context. The edge Sahagún describes depends on upbringing and discipline, not on fate. Interpretation

5. 819-day count

Correction. Earlier drafts placed the stations at Long Count day ≡ 3 mod 819, which put him in the South-yellow quarter. That was wrong.

Stations always fall on tzolk'in coefficient 1. The station just before the era base is 12.19.19.17.17 1 Kaban (day −3), so the stations sit at day ≡ −3 mod 819 Source: Berlin & Kelley 1961; Thompson; mayan-calendar.com. The direction comes from the station's day sign (Thompson's four groups): the Imix group is East/red, the Ik' group North/white, the Ak'bal group West/black and the K'an group South/yellow. Stations step E→S→W→N.

  • His birth: day 319 after that station (317 under correlation 584285; both correlations give the same quarter) Calculated.

What this means for you. The 819-day count belongs to K'awiil, the god of lineage, sustenance and the royal sceptre. Your station is the white north. In Maya thought the north is "up" (xaman), the zenith side where the ancestors and the sun at its height live. White (sak) is the colour of the pure, the new and the bright.

You were born late in this station, with 500 days left before the count turns to the red east. That makes you a carrier of inherited, ancestral authority (white north) who arrives just before a new dawn (the red east of both your day sign and the next station). The 13th-child marker (born the 13th, youngest of 13)

Calculated Tzolk'in 8 Kaban, in the trecena 1 Ok; Aztec trecena 1 Itzcuintli, the 14th, patron Xipe Totec (§9).

[Tradition] The 260-day count runs the numbers 1–13 against twenty day-signs; the Nahua sky had thirteen heavens with thirteen Lords of the Day; the Maya Long Count era is 13 baktuns, completed at 13.0.0.0.0 on 21 December 2012.

Interpretation What he carries. Thirteen is the full count of the Mesoamerican numbers: the thirteenth day completes a trecena, the thirteenth heaven is the top of the sky (Omeyocan in the cosmologies that count thirteen), and 13 baktuns complete an age. As the thirteenth child he is the completed trecena of his family, and he was born within the closing katuns of that 13-baktun age, fourteen years before 13.0.0.0.0. His own trecena's patron is Xipe Totec, lord of renewal, the god of the shed skin and the new growth.

Integrated Reading Interpretation

His Mesoamerican signature is 8 Kaban / 8 No'j / 8 Ollin: the earth that moves, ruled by thought. Each layer adds to it: - The Aztec layer sets Tlaloc by day and Chalchiuhtlicue by night, the rain-and-water couple who make things grow, inside a Xipe Totec trecena of renewal. - The body man puts his power in his tongue, so speech and counsel are his tools. - The Maya layer adds G1, a beginning night, and a red-east day. The corrected 819-day count and the codex year bearer (Muluk) both place his ancestral frame in the white north, K'awiil's quarter of lineage. He inherits from the north and acts toward the east. - His Lunar Series carves the Moon goddess at 1C over a 23-day waning moon: a finisher and repairer. - In the sky, Venus was hidden at his birth, while the Dresden table's own count put it on page 46 as a late Morning Star. The codex shows a public role at full readiness; the sky keeps it in reserve. Mars was rising. He is a figure of delayed but strong arrival. - The prophetic texts agree on the white mantle and the green quetzal, both of which he carries symbolically. The south dawn comes through his Tochtli year-bearer, and the quetzal through his year number 12. The Paris page of his birth katun is lost, but the page of his midlife katun, 13 Ahau (p.3), survives. - He belongs to the closing generation of b'ak'tun 12. He was born in Katun 4 Ahau, the katun of Kukulcan's second coming in the Chilam Balam, and the next "returning lord" katun, 13 Ahau, covers his years 2032–2052.

The tradition reads him as a counsellor-builder: practical, mobile, persuasive, and someone who turns ideas into ground-shaking change.
