6. The Perfect Nature (al-Ṭibāʿ al-Tāmm, III.6), your daemon

The Picatrix (III.6, Source, verify) calls the Perfect Nature "the spirit of the philosopher, linked to the planet that governs him", "conjoined with the virtue of the planet ruling the radix of the nativity". Its four names are Tamāġīs (تماغيس), Baġdīswād (بغديسواد), Waġdās (وغداس) and Nūfānāġādīs (نوفاناغاديس); in Latin, Meegius, Betzahuech, Vacdez, Nufeneguediz. The rite is performed with the Moon in the first degree of Aries: four pitchers of oils or butter, four of wine, a candle, frankincense or mastic and aloes, and an invocation facing east.

Your Perfect Nature Interpretation: a Jupiterian Perfect Nature, with Mercury as its key and Venus as its almuten-ally. Jupiter is lord of the Ascendant, in the Ascendant, in its own sign and face, and in sect; this is exactly the "planet ruling the radix". Mercury rules the Lot of the Daimon and the hour of birth. Venus is the almuten figuris. What this means for you: in this book's reading, your guiding twin-spirit takes the shape of a generous, enthroned teacher (Jupiter) who speaks through writing (Mercury). It is reached through ordered study and good company, not through force.
