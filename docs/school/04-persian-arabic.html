1. The tradition and its sources

Persian-Arabic astrology runs from Sasanian court astrology (Pahlavi translations of Dorotheus and Vettius Valens, the Zij-i Shahriyar, the Zoroastrian millennial scheme) into Abbasid Baghdad. There, Masha'allah (d. c. 815), Sahl ibn Bishr, 'Umar al-Tabari, Abu Ma'shar (787–886), al-Qabisi (10th c.) and al-Biruni (Kitab al-Tafhim, 1029) gave the art its classic shape. Its signature tools are reception and the strict rules of 'application', the many lots (sihām), the firdaria, the tasyir (directing through the bounds), the doctrine of Saturn–Jupiter conjunctions, and the 28 lunar mansions (manāzil al-qamar). Next to it sit letter-science ('ilm al-ḥurūf, abjad) and the end-times literature of hadith, Shi'i ghayba works and the Pahlavi apocalypses. Sources I checked online this round: Abu Dawud 4282–4286, Muslim 2937, Muslim 156 and Ibn Majah 4082 (graded Da'if) on sunnah.com; al-Biruni's lots in the Ramsay Wright translation (skyscript.co.uk/alparts.html); the IMO meteor-shower calendar. Other citations are marked Source, verify.

Mercury — weakness: Peregrine in Scorpio; under the rays (12°27'); opposite Saturn. Rules that fire: Mutual reception with Mars by domicile + applying sextile 1°06'; WS 9th (house of religion, wisdom). Net verdict: REVERSED-STRONG. Interpretation

What this means for you. A mind that looks hemmed in becomes one of your best tools once Mars takes its side. Your speech has force.

Mars — weakness: Peregrine (participating triplicity only); out of sect. Rules that fire: Mutual reception with Mercury by domicile + applying sextile; WS 7th angle; trine Saturn. Net verdict: REVERSED-STRONG. Interpretation

What this means for you. Your drive is not wild. It serves your intellect, and that makes it reliable.

Jupiter — weakness: Retrograde. Rules that fire: Own domicile + face; WS 1st, 0°57' from Asc; Asc lord & MC lord. Net verdict: STRONG (retrograde delays, does not cancel). Interpretation

What this means for you. Your benefic is fully strong. Retrograde means its gifts arrive after reflection or a second attempt, not that they are lost.

Saturn — weakness: Peregrine, retrograde, cadent (WS 3rd). Rules that fire: In sect (diurnal, day chart). Venus-Saturn domicile/exaltation exchange exists but in aversion, so per Sahl reception does not fire. Net verdict: WEAK (sect only mitigates). Interpretation

What this means for you. Saturn gives you patience but little outside help. Its lessons come through slow, solitary effort, and they are lasting.
