1. Dane Rudhyar: humanistic astrology

Source Rudhyar described humanistic astrology as "person-centered": the chart is "a set of instructions for the fulfillment of a potential," not a list of events (Rudhyar, The Astrology of Personality, 1936; summary at humanisticastrology.com/rudhyar). His core tools are the lunation cycle, the progressed lunation, planetary pair cycles, and the Sabian symbols reread as a 360-step cycle.

1.1 Birth lunation phase

Calculated The Moon is 287.18° ahead of the Sun. That falls in the Last Quarter phase (270–315°). Source Rudhyar, The Lunation Cycle (1967), via khaldea.com/rudhyar/astroarticles/lunationguidance.php. The Last Quarter type shows "managerial and organizing ability in terms of broad social ideals...; an eagerness to force issues and precipitate crises, to reform and transform, to build new systems and to work hard for future goals regardless of personal cost; humor."

What this means for you: You were born in the reforming half of the cycle. You see what already exists and judge it against an ideal: the old has to be reorganised for something that is coming. You are wired to build systems, not just enjoy them. You will push a situation to a decision rather than let it drift. Your humour lets you criticise without bitterness. The cost Rudhyar names, working for the future "regardless of personal cost", is your typical over-reach.
