0. How the Golden Dawn did astrology

Source The Order did not have a separate horoscopic system. It took the astrology of its day (tropical, Lilly-style dignities, Ptolemaic aspects), taught it in the Knowledge Lectures, then re-mapped every degree onto the Tree of Life: through the Hebrew letters of the Sepher Yetzirah, the Tarot of Book T, the 72 angels of the Shem ha-Mephorash, the colour scales, the tattvas, geomancy and the ritual figures (pentagram, hexagram, heptagram). So a GD reading is a chart read as a map of paths and powers to be worked with in ritual, not just a set of character statements. Book T adds its own zodiac: the first degree of Leo is fixed at the star Regulus (Cor Leonis). That puts the Book T zodiac about 29°48′ behind the tropical one for 1998.

Source The Zelator and Theoricus lectures give the domiciles, exaltations, detriments and falls (Saturn: Capricorn/Aquarius, exalted in Libra; Jupiter: Sagittarius/Pisces, exalted in Cancer; Mars: Aries/Scorpio, exalted in Capricorn; Sun: Leo, exalted in Aries; Venus: Taurus/Libra, exalted in Pisces; Mercury: Gemini/Virgo, exalted in Virgo; Moon: Cancer, exalted in Taurus). Placement first, then hidden strength, then the net verdict.

Sun, 20°20′ Libra, 7th house. Placement: in fall (Libra is opposite the Sun's exaltation). Hidden strength: angular (on the 7th side of the horizon); the sect light of a day chart; disposited by Venus, which is in her own sign in the same house; sextile the MC. Book T layer: the Sun moves to Virgo, out of fall. Net verdict: weakness cancelled. The Sun is weak in its sign but strong by position and well held by its ruler. What this means for you: the will does not stand alone. It turns on as soon as there is a counterpart, a client, a partner or an opponent. Alone you hesitate; in relationship you lead.

Moon, 7°31′ Leo, 5th house. Placement: peregrine, succedent. Hidden strength: in the Book T zodiac the Moon is at 7°42′ Cancer, her own sign; trine Pluto (1°18′). Under strain: opposite Uranus, square Mercury and Saturn. Net verdict: strong in shadow. Weak on the surface tropically, and dignified in the Order's own star-fixed zodiac. What this means for you: the emotional life looks theatrical and proud (Leo, the 5th house) but underneath it is protective and domestic (Cancer). People see the performance. Those close to you meet the caretaker.

Mercury, 2°47′ Scorpio, 7th house. Placement: peregrine, angular. Hidden strength: mutual reception with Mars (Mercury in Mars's sign, Mars in Mercury's sign and exaltation, Virgo); sextile Mars (1°06′). Under strain: opposite Saturn and square Neptune. Net verdict: strong (cancelled weakness). What this means for you: a penetrating, investigative mind that works best in dialogue and negotiation. It argues with itself (Mercury opposite Saturn) until a position is airtight.

Venus, 16°08′ Libra, 7th house. Placement: domicile, angular. Hidden strength: final dispositor of the Sun, Saturn and the 7th house; Saturn is received, because Venus sits in Saturn's exaltation. Book T: Venus at 16°20′ Virgo, her fall, so the second layer warns. Net verdict: strong. This is the best-placed personal planet. What this means for you: tact, aesthetic judgement and fairness are your natural tools, and partnerships tend to bring benefit.

Mars, 3°53′ Virgo, 6th house. Placement: peregrine, cadent, in a hard house. Hidden strength: mutual reception with Mercury; trine Saturn (2°56′); Mars is the ruler of the day of birth (Tuesday). Book T: Mars moves to Leo, a fire sign, more at home. Under strain: square Pluto. Net verdict: weakness cancelled. What this means for you: the drive goes into craft, routine and precision rather than open fights. You fight by doing the work better.

Jupiter, 19°42′ Pisces ℞, conjunct Ascendant. Placement: domicile, effectively on the 1st cusp (0°56′ inside the 12th but within Lilly's 5° rule for the next cusp). Hidden strength: chart ruler (traditional ruler of Pisces), final dispositor of the Ascendant, the MC and Neptune's sign line. Weakness: retrograde. Book T: Jupiter moves to Aquarius, Saturn's sign. Net verdict: strong, inward-turned. What this means for you: the single most important planet. You are recognised by your faith, your kindness and a sense that things will turn out right. Because Jupiter is retrograde, the gift matures slowly and turns inward first: belief has to be earned before it can be handed on.

Saturn, 0°57′ Taurus ℞, 1st house. Placement: peregrine, angular. Hidden strength: mixed mutual reception with Venus (Saturn in Venus's house, Venus in Saturn's exaltation); trine Mars and trine the MC. Under strain: opposite Mercury and square Neptune. Book T: Saturn at 1°08′ Aries, its fall. Net verdict: strong in shadow, by reception. What this means for you: a serious, stubborn and durable core under the gentle Pisces surface. In the second layer it is fragile: patience is something you build, not something you were handed.

Outer planets (not GD rulers; the Order's papers predate wide use of them, so they are read through their signs and paths only). Uranus in Aquarius and Neptune at the end of Capricorn, both in the 11th: a generation's signature put in the house of groups and hopes. Pluto in Sagittarius in the 9th: conviction, foreign or philosophical intensity.

Synthesis, §2. Two essentially dignified planets (Venus and Jupiter) and two mutual receptions (Mercury with Mars; Saturn with Venus). No planet in this chart is unsupported. The weak spots (Sun in fall, a peregrine Moon) are each rescued, one by angularity and reception, the other by the Book T zodiac. The GD would call this a balanced chart in which Netzach (Venus) and Chesed (Jupiter) carry the load, the right-hand Pillar of Mercy.
