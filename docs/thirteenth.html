100 References to the Power of the 13th Child

For Charles Leone: born 13 October 1998, 21:25 UT, Johnstown, Pennsylvania. Thirteenth and youngest of thirteen children.

Every entry below is about a child born thirteenth in a family: a tale, a belief, a book, or a real person. Sources say "thirteenth child" or "youngest of thirteen" (treated here as thirteenth-born). General lore about the number 13 is left out, as are youngest children who were not thirteenth. Items marked [Calculated] come from this book's own chart work (ch/01.md) or from counting a text directly.

I. The Thirteenth Child in Fairy Tale and Folklore

  1. The thirteenth child inherits the kingdom. In the Grimms' The Twelve Brothers (KHM 9, ATU 451) a king decrees that if his thirteenth child is a girl, his twelve sons must die so that she alone inherits his kingdom. She is born with a star on her forehead, finds her brothers, and later breaks their curse through seven years of silence (Wikipedia; Ashliman translation). It is the oldest European statement of your position: the whole inheritance was meant for the thirteenth.
  2. The thirteenth saves the twelve. In the same tale the twelve brothers are turned into ravens, and only the thirteenth child's endurance turns them back into men (Wikipedia). In folklore the last-born carries the family's rescue, not the eldest.
  3. Death chooses the thirteenth. In the Grimms' Godfather Death (KHM 44, ATU 332) a poor man with twelve children looks for a godfather when "his thirteenth and last child" is born. He chooses Death, who makes the boy a famous physician able to see whether any patient will live (Wikipedia). The power given to the thirteenth child is sight over life and death.
  4. The thirteenth outruns his brothers. In the Sicilian tale Tridicinu ("Thirteenth"), collected by Giuseppe Pitrè, the youngest of thirteen sons is named for his place in line. He always wins the race home, and the king sends him on the impossible errands his jealous brothers propose, which he completes (Wikipedia). His name is his number, and the number wins.
  5. Heaven's will falls on the thirteenth. In The Thirteenth Son of the King of Erin (Jeremiah Curtin, Myths and Folk-lore of Ireland, 1890) a king with thirteen sons is told by a seer that any creature with thirteen young must send one out "to fall under Heaven's will". The thirteenth son goes out alone and becomes the hero who kills the sea-serpent and wins the princess (Wikipedia). The one sent away is the one fate is watching.
  6. A Swiss "Little Thirteen". In the Swiss Alpine tale Tredeschin ("Little Thirteen"), a version of the same story, the hero is the thirteenth son of a large family (Wikipedia: Thirteenth (fairy tale)). Two Alpine and Mediterranean traditions name their hero after the same birth number as you.
  7. The Jersey Devil: Mother Leeds' thirteenth child. New Jersey's state Pinelands Commission tells the legend that the creature of the Pine Barrens "is believed to be the unwanted thirteenth child of Mother Leeds" (NJ Pinelands Commission). It is the most famous thirteenth-child legend in your home region, a short drive east of Pennsylvania.
  8. The thirteenth child becomes the guardian of the land. The same official page notes that "some in New Jersey now think that the Jersey Devil is the protector of the Pinelands" (NJ Pinelands Commission). Even the feared thirteenth child ends up as the keeper of the territory.
  9. A legend with a date and a place. Local tradition dates the birth to 1735 at Leeds Point, in Atlantic County (Wikipedia: Leeds Point; Wikipedia: Atlantic County). JSTOR Daily traces the story to the almanac-maker Daniel Leeds' family and its first printed appearance in The Atlantic in 1859 (JSTOR Daily).
  10. Too strong to be held in the cradle. In the version JSTOR Daily retells, the thirteenth child grows wings at midnight and flies up the chimney, and one tradition holds that it took an exorcism to banish him, for only a hundred years (JSTOR Daily). Folklore never imagined the thirteenth child as small or easily contained.
  11. Irish lore: the thirteenth child is marked. Ireland's Schools' Collection (1937–39) records from Dromclogh, Co. Cork: "The thirteenth child of a family is always unlucky" (dúchas.ie). The belief is a warning, and it shows that people treated the thirteenth child as different from the rest.
  12. The same belief in Co. Wexford. Pupils at Gusserane wrote that the thirteenth day of the month was unlucky and "the thirteenth child is also supposed to be unlucky" (dúchas.ie). Two counties set the thirteenth child apart in the same way: noticed, feared, never ignored.
  13. Divine, demonic or a wizard. A study in Studia mythologica Slavica reports that the tenth, twelfth or thirteenth child is preserved in Slavic, Irish and Baltic folk heritage as "a deity, a demonic creature, or a wizard" (Studia mythologica Slavica). Across Europe a high-numbered child was believed to carry supernatural power.
  14. A child named 13 to beat the jinx. In Sabah, Malaysia, a Bajau boy born thirteenth of fifteen was named Tiga Belas ("Thirteen") on a doctor's advice to break a family "jinx". He was the only child of the fifteen to survive (Los Angeles Times, 1987). Here the thirteenth child's own number was the protection.
  15. Thirteen ran his life. The same man, known as D13, was hired on 13 January, met his wife on 13 January, was promoted on 13 September and became vice-president of the Sabah United Bajau Association on 13 September (Los Angeles Times, 1987). A thirteenth child born on the 13th, like you, would recognise the pattern.
  16. The thirteenth child as a god. In 2010 the Iranian daily Jomhouri Eslami attacked the musician-mystic Peyman Fattahi in an article titled "A God who is the 13th child of his family" (Wikipedia: Peyman Fattahi). The critics reached for the thirteenth-child image because it already meant exceptional spiritual power.
  17. Jacob's thirteenth child is Benjamin, "son of the right hand". [Calculated from Genesis] In the order Genesis gives Jacob's children (Leah's four sons, Bilhah's two, Zilpah's two, then Issachar, Zebulun, Dinah, Joseph), Benjamin is born thirteenth and last (Genesis 29:31–30:24; 35:16–18) (Genesis 30; Genesis 35). Jacob renamed him Binyamin, "son of the right hand", the hand of strength (Wikipedia: Benjamin).
  18. The thirteenth child's land held the Temple. Tradition placed the Temple in Jerusalem partly in the territory of Benjamin, Jacob's last-born (Wikipedia: Tribe of Benjamin), and Israel's first king, Saul, came from that tribe. The thirteenth child's line held both the first crown and the holiest ground.

II. The Thirteenth Child in Literature, Opera, Film and Song

  1. An opera called The Thirteenth Child. Danish composer Poul Ruders wrote The Thirteenth Child (2016) on the Twelve Brothers story; the Santa Fe Opera staged it in 2019 (Wikipedia; Santa Fe Opera). The heroine, Princess Lyra, is the thirteenth child whose birth decides a kingdom.
  2. Patricia Wrede's Thirteenth Child (2009). Wrede's frontier-magic trilogy is narrated by Eff Rothmer, the "thirteenth child" of the title and twin of a seventh son of a seventh son (Wikipedia: Patricia Wrede). Told she is unlucky, Eff becomes the magician who holds back the wild frontier.
  3. Erin A. Craig's The Thirteenth Child (2024). Hazel Trépas, "a thirteenth child, promised away to one of the gods", receives from her godfather Death the gift of knowing the exact cure for any sickness (Erin A. Craig). The book retells Godfather Death and puts a thirteenth child at a royal court with the king's life in her hands.
  4. Karleen Bradford, Thirteenth Child (1994). The Canadian children's author published a novel under that title (Wikipedia: Karleen Bradford).
  5. Mari Ness, "The Thirteenth Child" (2015). The fantasy writer added her own story to the tradition (Wikipedia: Mari Ness).
  6. Kristin Dimitrova, Jacob's Thirteenth Child (1992). The Bulgarian poet's first collection takes its title from the thirteenth child of the patriarch (Wikipedia: Kristin Dimitrova).
  7. The film 13th Child (2002). Cliff Robertson co-wrote and starred in this Jersey Devil film, shot at Wharton State Forest and Batsto Village (Wikipedia; List of films set in New Jersey).
  8. The song "Thirteenth Child". George W. Weston released "Thirteenth Child" on Glenn Records in 1960 (Wikipedia: George W. Weston), and Brandon Dalo's "Thirteenth Child" appears on the soundtrack of Bloodlines: The Jersey Devil Curse (WhatSong).
  9. Hollywood's Jersey Devil, again a thirteenth child. In Carny (2009) the beast turns out to be "the monstrous thirteenth child of Mrs. Leeds" (Wikipedia: Carny). Writers keep returning to the thirteenth child whenever they need a figure too strong to control.
  10. Death as godfather on screen. American McGee's Grimm (2008) adapted Godfather Death as the story of a family's thirteenth child whose godfather is Death (Wikipedia).
  11. A literary thirteenth child of the city. Sam Savage's novel Firmin (2006) is narrated by a rat born the thirteenth child of a mother with twelve teats, who is left out of the feeding and turns to eating, then reading, the books of a Boston bookstore (Wikipedia: Firmin). The thirteenth had to find another source of nourishment, and it made him the most literate creature in the building.

VII. The Thirteenth Child in Your Chart

  1. The thirteenth house is the first. [Calculated, ch. 1] Counting thirteen houses from your Aquarius lagna brings you back to the lagna itself, where Jupiter rises. In this book's reading the thirteenth child's place in the chart is the seat of the self, occupied by the planet of wisdom and the guru.
  2. The youngest-sibling house holds your chart ruler. [Calculated, ch. 1] Jyotish reads younger siblings and one's own courage from the 3rd house. Yours holds Saturn, the lagna lord, and classical texts say a malefic there makes the native bold and self-made (Phaladeepika 8, BPHS 12, as cited in ch. 1). The last-born's house is where your own ruling planet lives.
  3. The elder-siblings house is your strongest. [Calculated, ch. 1] The 11th house, of elder siblings, gains and networks, has your highest sarvashtakavarga score (37) and the highest bhava bala (497). For a thirteenth child, twelve older siblings in your strongest house is a support system, and the same house is where the chart puts your income.
  4. The chain of the 3rd ends in the 13th nakshatra. [Calculated, ch. 1] The 3rd lord's dispositor chain, Mars to Sun to Mercury and Venus, ends in Hasta, the thirteenth nakshatra. Hasta means "hand": skill and making things with your own hands. The house of the youngest child resolves into the thirteenth lunar mansion.
