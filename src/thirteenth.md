## I. The Day Itself: Calendars and Sky

1. **You were born on Simchat Torah.** Tuesday 13 October 1998 was 23 Tishrei 5759, Simchat Torah in the Diaspora, ending at nightfall that evening ([Hebcal](https://www.hebcal.com/holidays/simchat-torah-1998)). You arrived on the festival of "Rejoicing in the Torah."

It is a day past the complete set, the same way a thirteenth child comes after a full twelve.
4. **The Hebrew year can have thirteen months.** Seven years in every nineteen-year cycle add a second Adar, giving thirteen months ([Wikipedia: Hebrew calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_calendar)). Thirteen is how the Jewish calendar realigns itself with the sun.

11. **Some years have thirteen full moons.** A solar year is about 12.4 lunar months, so roughly every two to three years a thirteenth full moon appears. ## IX. Mesoamerica

70. **Healer of skin and eyes.** Sahagún's informants say Xipe sent skin and eye diseases and could cure them ([Mikulska](https://doi.org/10.1017/s0956536120000553)). That is another healer thread, alongside the Ashvins and Dhanvantari.
71. **Thirteen heavens.** Aztec cosmology layered the upper world into thirteen heavens ([Wikipedia: Thirteen Heavens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirteen_Heavens)). Thirteen is the ladder up.
72. **The thirteenth baktun closed in your childhood.** The Maya Long Count completed 13 baktuns on 21 December 2012 ([Wikipedia: Long Count](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesoamerican_Long_Count_calendar)), when you were 14. 90. **Friday the 13th is a 19th-century superstition.** The earliest records are French, from the 1830s. Thomas Lawson's 1907 novel *Friday, the Thirteenth* spread it in America ([Library of Congress Folklife](https://blogs.loc.gov/folklife/2017/01/whos-afraid-of-friday-the-thirteenth/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friday_the_13th)).
91. **The Templars were arrested on your birthday, and the "Friday 13th" origin story is modern.** Philip IV's officers seized the Knights Templar on Friday 13 October 1307 ([Wikipedia: Knights Templar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knights_Templar)). Linking it to the Friday-the-13th superstition is a recent popular theory.
92. **"Triskaidekaphobia" dates from 1910.** The word appears in Isador Coriat's *Abnormal Psychology* ([Wikipedia: Triskaidekaphobia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triskaidekaphobia)).
93. **The Thirteen Club.** In 1882 Captain William Fowler founded a New York dining club that met 13 at a table on the 13th to mock the superstition ([Library of Congress Folklife](https://blogs.loc.gov/folklife/2017/01/whos-afraid-of-friday-the-thirteenth/)).
94. **The baker's dozen.** Thirteen for twelve is usually traced to medieval English bread laws that punished short weight, though the exact origin is debated ([Wikipedia: Baker's dozen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baker%27s_dozen)). The thirteenth is the generous extra.
95. **Sizdah Bedar: send the thirteenth day outdoors.** On the 13th day of Nowruz, Iranians picnic outside and throw away sprouted greens to cast off the year's bad luck ([Wikipedia: Sizdah Bedar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sizdah_Bedar)).
96. **Grimm: "The Twelve Brothers."** A king vows that if his thirteenth child is a girl, his twelve sons must die. The daughter later searches for her brothers and redeems them ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Twelve_Brothers); [Grimm Stories text](https://www.grimmstories.com/en/grimm_fairy-tales/pdf/the_twelve_brothers.pdf)). In Grimm, the thirteenth child is the one who saves the siblings.
97. **Grimm: "Briar Rose" and the thirteenth wise woman.** The king owns only twelve golden plates, so the thirteenth wise woman is left out and curses the princess ([Wikipedia: Sleeping Beauty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Beauty)). The thirteenth is powerful because she was overlooked.
98. **Argentina's presidential godchildren.** Since 1907, a seventh son (and since 1973 a seventh daughter) can become the president's godchild. The custom came from Volga German immigrants; the "werewolf" explanation is a later conflation ([The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2014/dec/29/argentina-kirchner-adopt-child-werewolf)). As a 13th child you are well past the seventh, in folklore's language a child of surplus.

## XV. Birth Order, History and Famous Thirteenths

99. **Birth order, the lastborn and the science.** Alfred Adler put birth order at the centre of personality ([Wikipedia: Alfred Adler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Adler)), and Frank Sulloway's *Born to Rebel* argued that later-borns are the innovators ([Wikipedia: Frank Sulloway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Sulloway)). The largest modern study (Rohrer et al., *PNAS* 2015, about 20,000 people) found birth order's measurable trace is in intellect, not temperament ([doi](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1506451112)). Strachan's 1989 *BMJ* paper also found hay fever less common in children with many older siblings, the seed of the "hygiene hypothesis" ([doi](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.299.6710.1259); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hygiene_hypothesis)). Twelve older siblings is about as much "hygiene hypothesis" as a childhood can hold.
100. **The youngest inherits the hearth, and the thirteenth-born keep making history.** Under ultimogeniture and the English custom of Borough English, the youngest son inherited the family home ([Wikipedia: Borough English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borough_English); [Ultimogeniture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultimogeniture)). Among the Mongols the youngest son, Tolui, kept his father Genghis Khan's home base and became regent ([Wikipedia: Tolui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tolui)). Famous thirteenths include **Josiah Wedgwood**, "thirteenth and last child," just like you ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josiah_Wedgwood)); **Ignatius of Loyola**, youngest of thirteen and founder of the Jesuits ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ignatius_of_Loyola)); and **Archduke Johann of Austria**, thirteenth child of Emperor Leopold II ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archduke_John_of_Austria)). Your own date, 13 October, is the day the White House cornerstone was laid (1792; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_House)) and the day the International Meridian Conference voted for Greenwich as the prime meridian (1884; [conference proceedings, Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/17759/17759-h/17759-h.htm)). It was a day for laying foundations and setting the zero point. Your birthplace is Johnstown, marked by the 1889 flood that killed 2,209 people ([Wikipedia: Johnstown Flood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnstown_Flood)), and your nation began as thirteen colonies ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirteen_Colonies)) whose Thirteenth Amendment ended slavery ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirteenth_Amendment_to_the_United_States_Constitution)). For a gui-water child, that history weighs heavily, and the thirteen-colony one is about beginning again.
